Chapter 9

Notifications

Until this round each notification was its own piece of code, and most moments passed in silence: nobody told the PM a permit was about to expire, nobody told the dealer their project moved, and there was no list of what had been sent to whom. Migration 004700 makes notifications one system: a catalogue of 55 kinds, a feed per person, email and push, and an admin screen with a switch on every one.

How it works

1 · Something happensA trigger on the business table (projects, the seven stage forms, documents, chat, e-sign, change orders, contacts, deals, ratings, commissions, exceptions) raises the kind with a payload.
2 · Or time passesThe scheduled job raises ageing, expiring permits, tomorrow's unready installs, quiet contacts and quiet deals.
3 · Once eachA kind and key is raised once per recipient, so a project moved back and forth, or a job run twice, sends nothing twice.
4 · DeliveredEvery ten minutes: the words are rendered once (feed, email and push read the same), emailed unless the homeowner opted out, pushed to registered devices. Homeowners' rows wait through quiet hours; staff's do not.
5 · SeenA bell in the sidebar with the unread count; /notifications for staff and dealers; Updates in the homeowner's portal and app.

A homeowner with no login is emailed; one who opted out of email and has no login is not told at all; one with the app is told in it. Each kind has three switches — in app, email, push — and an On switch that stops it being raised at all. The timing settings (days before a contact or deal counts as quiet, permit warning window, briefing hour) sit on the same screen, Admin → Notifications.

The scheduled job

One endpoint, /api/push/reminders, does everything time-based: install reminders, the quiet-hours flush, the chat and rating digests, the timed notification rules, notification delivery, and (chapter 10) the AI queue. vercel.json now runs it every ten minutes with CRON_SECRET; an admin can also press Run now. Every part of it is safe to run twice.

One thing to check

Vercel's Hobby plan allows a cron to run once a day. The ten-minute schedule needs the Pro plan; otherwise change the schedule in vercel.json to daily and use an outside scheduler (GitHub Actions, cron-job.org, or any server's cron) to call the endpoint with Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET> every ten minutes.

Chapter 10

AI automation

Ask SolarFlow answered questions. This round gives the same model work to do on its own, in the shape the blueprint asked for: small jobs the database records, a confidence on everything, an exceptions queue for doubt, and every switch starting on the cautious side. Migration 004800; Admin → AI automation; the Exceptions screen in the sidebar.

What runs

JobWhat happensThe person's part
Read a stage attachment D1 · D2Every PDF or photo attached to a stage form is read by the model, which is told the stage and its fields and asked for the values it can see, each with a confidence and the words that support it. A permit letter yields the permit number, approval date, expiry and status; a packing slip the PO number and delivery date; a plan set the final system size and module count. Invalid values and unknown fields are dropped. A file that is not what it was filed as (a dog photo as an HOA approval) becomes a high-severity exception with nothing proposed.Suggestions appear on the stage form ("Read from the attachments") and in the exceptions queue with the evidence beside each. Accept writes the value through the form's own allowlist; Reject writes nothing. With auto-apply on, values above the threshold are written at once and only the rest ask.
Evidence-based auto-advance D3No model involved. For each stage the admin has ticked, a project whose form is complete and whose required attachments are in moves to the next stage through moveProject — the same gate, the same homeowner notification, the same chat line and the same audit entry as the green button, attributed to the automation. Held back while any suggested value on the project is undecided.Tick the stages on Admin → AI automation. Start with Inspection & PTO and Permits.

Money, cancellation, complaints and dates that are not set always need a person; the draft is then a short holding reply.The draft sits above the composer with Send to Hana Home, an editable box, and Dismiss. Morning briefing O3At the chosen hour, Ask SolarFlow writes each project manager's briefing from the PM report, under that PM's own permissions: the two or three things to do today, what is ageing and why, what is blocked on the customer, messages waiting, the next seven days. Once per person per day.It arrives in the feed and by email (Admin → Notifications decides the channels).

The exceptions queue B3

A screen over the exceptions table, for admin and ops. Each card shows the severity, the project, what the reader found and its notes, a link to the document, and the pending suggestions as a table with Accept / Reject on each row and one button for all. Acknowledge, Dismiss and Resolve on every exception; resolving closes undecided suggestions without applying them. When the last suggestion from a document is decided — here or on the stage form — its exception resolves itself. A new exception raises the PM's "AI needs a decision" notification.

Why it is safe

The form's own allowlist

An accepted or auto-applied value goes through exactly the code the stage form's Save uses: the field must exist on that stage, the value must fit its type and options. The model cannot write a column the form does not have.

Documents are data, never instructions

The reading job has no tools that act. It returns a schema — fields, confidences, evidence, issues — and the application decides what to do with it under the admin's switches.

A real identity, a full record

The automation acts as a named service account with no password, hidden from every list. Its moves appear in the stage history and the activity log as its own, not a PM's; every read, suggestion, decision and automatic reply is logged with tokens used.

Idempotent and retry-safe

A document is read once, a message answered once, a briefing written once a day, however often the job runs. Transient API errors retry with a delay; after three attempts a job fails for good and says why on the admin screen.

Cost

The jobs run at low effort on Claude Opus 5 by default (AI_EFFORT raises it). Reading a one-page letter costs a few cents; a plan set tens of cents; a reply draft two to five cents; a briefing ten to twenty cents per PM per day. The activity log records the real tokens for every job, so the actual figure is a report away.
