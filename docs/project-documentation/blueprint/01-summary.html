Chapter 1

Summary

Seven things have been built across two rounds, each tested end to end: stage attachments, PandaDoc e-signature, change orders and Ask SolarFlow first; then the solar facts on every stage form, a notification system for every role, and the first AI automation — a document reader, reply drafts, morning briefings and evidence-based auto-advance, with an exceptions queue for whatever the system is unsure of. Behind them sits the plan this document exists for: automations that take the routine work out of every stage, so that people handle exceptions and customers rather than paperwork.

Delivered

Stage attachments Live

The Drive Updated tick is gone. Each stage form now has an Attachments card, with that stage's files: survey photos, plan set, permit and ICA approvals, inspection report, PTO letter and so on. A project cannot advance until the required files are actually on it. Stages already closed under the old tick stay closed. A fix to stage-form uploads came with it.

PandaDoc e-signature Live · needs keys

From the signing form, the contract goes to the homeowner by email, or is signed on screen with them present. When they sign, the project is created exactly as if the rep had pressed Sign, and the signed PDF is filed on it. Webhooks are verified, completion is idempotent, and every failure leaves a reason with a Finish button.

Change orders Live

A panel on every project: raise a change order with its reason and amount, send it for signature through PandaDoc, or approve it by hand. Once it is signed or approved, the amount is added to the contract value, once, and the signed PDF is linked from the change order.

Ask SolarFlow Live · needs key

An AI assistant in the sidebar of every staff and dealer screen. Ask "give me the PM report" or "which projects are stuck in Permits, and why?" and it answers from live data. It runs on Claude Opus 5, reads through eight tools and never sees more than the asker could, because every lookup runs under that person's own database permissions.

Stage fields Live

Every stage form now records the facts a solar job turns on: roof and main panel at Survey, the final system at Design, permit numbers and expiry, PO and tracking, crew, inspector, monitoring site, close-out. Typed columns, all optional, all report fields — and the exact place the document reader writes to (chapter 8).

Notifications Live

One catalogue of 55 kinds for homeowners, PMs, admins, reps and dealers — permit approved, equipment delivered, install tomorrow not ready, your project moved — raised by the database once each, delivered in the app, by email and by push, held through quiet hours for homeowners, with a switch on every one under Admin → Notifications. The scheduled job is now in vercel.json (chapter 9).

AI automation Live · needs key

Every stage attachment is read into suggestions with evidence for a person to accept (or written at once above a threshold, if allowed); homeowners' messages get a drafted reply; each PM gets a morning briefing; complete projects move on by themselves for the stages the admin ticks; and an Exceptions screen holds everything the system would not decide alone (chapter 10).

Along the way, a real bug surfaced and was fixed: a sales rep's dealer list on the signing form was always empty, because the dealers table is closed to sales. A dealer directory now provides names and nothing else (migration 004500).

The blueprint in one page

The recommendations follow one idea: every stage should finish itself when the evidence arrives. A document or a status update is read, checked against the stage's rules, and the project moves on, with a person asked only when the evidence is missing, unclear or contradicts itself. SolarFlow is well placed for this. The stage rules, the permissions and the audit trail already live in the database, so an automation acting through them gets the same refusals a person would.

Phases over ~9 months

The ten with the most value, in order

  1. Switch on the scheduler. Done The cron is in vercel.json; set CRON_SECRET (ch. 9, 11).
  2. Document AI on every upload. Built Every stage attachment is read into the form's fields with evidence; mismatched files are flagged (ch. 10). Next: the utility bill on the deal, and refiling mislabelled uploads (ch. 12).
  3. Evidence-based auto-advance. Built Per stage, off until ticked (ch. 10). Next: require the proving document's values to have been read, not just the form to be complete (ch. 12).
  4. Speed-to-lead in under 60 seconds. Instant SMS and email with a booking link, round-robin assignment, and reminders with no-show recovery (ch. 13).
  5. Utility-bill reader to proposal. A bill photo becomes usage, rate and utility on the deal, then a preliminary design and price (ch. 13).
  6. Auto-send the contract. On Financing approved, send the PandaDoc contract built from the proposal, with reminders until signed (ch. 13).
  7. Permit and interconnection watcher. Poll portals and inboxes for status changes, write them to the permit track, and chase what has gone quiet (ch. 15).
  8. Design-to-contract check. Compare the final design with the signed system and raise the change order automatically when they differ (ch. 14).
  9. Ask SolarFlow, v2. Let the assistant do things as well as answer (move a stage, ask the customer, raise a task), always with a confirm button (ch. 18).
  10. Homeowner copilot. Built Drafts every reply; sends confident routine ones itself when allowed, signed as automatic (ch. 10, 18).
