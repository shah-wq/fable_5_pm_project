Chapter 15

Across the office

The stage automations take work away. These make the remaining work faster: one place to ask and act, replies drafted from the project's own facts, and warnings before a project goes wrong rather than after.

RecommendationHow it works in SolarFlowPeople after
O1Ask SolarFlow can act, with a confirmHighMAdd action tools to the assistant: move a stage, put on hold with a reason, ask the customer for something, raise a task or change order, assign a PM, send the contract. Each action returns a proposal card ("Move PRJ-1A2B to Design? The survey photos are in.") and runs only when the person presses Confirm, through the same functions and permissions as the screens, logged as done by that person through the assistant."Put the Smith job on hold until the HOA answers" is one sentence and one click.
O2Homeowner copilotHighMWhen a homeowner writes in the project chat, a reply is drafted from the project's stage, dates, missing items and typical durations, in the company's tone. Start at L2 (the PM edits and sends). Move routine questions ("when is my install?", "what is PTO?") to L3 once the PM accepts most drafts unchanged. Complaints, money and anything unusual always go to the PM.The PM answers the few messages that need a person; the rest are answered in minutes, even at night.
O3Morning briefing per PMHighSAt 7 am each PM gets a short briefing (email, push, and the top of their dashboard): what moved overnight and why, what is ageing past threshold, what is blocked and on whom, customer messages waiting, today's installs and inspections with their readiness, and the exceptions to clear. It is built from the same tools as Ask SolarFlow.The day starts with a list, not an inbox.
O4Delay and cancellation early warningMediumMFrom the project's own history (days in stage compared with typical, a stage revisited, a low rating, unanswered messages, a financing stipulation, a quiet homeowner), each active project gets a risk score and its top reasons. The highest risks are flagged on the board, and a retention call is suggested before the customer cancels.PMs spend time on the projects at risk, not the loudest ones.
O5Dealer and rep insightsMediumSA monthly written summary per dealer and per rep, from the report builder: volume, conversion, cancellations and their reasons, cycle times, customer ratings, and changes from last month. Written in plain language, with the numbers linked to the report that produced them.Partner reviews are prepared automatically.
O6Cash and capacity forecastMediumMMilestone payments (down payment, M1–M3, finance M1/M2) projected from each project's stage and the typical time to reach each milestone, giving expected cash by week. Installs forecast the same way give crew capacity needed by week.Finance and scheduling plan from a forecast instead of a spreadsheet.
