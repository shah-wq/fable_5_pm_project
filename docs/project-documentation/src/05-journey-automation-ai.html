Chapter 10

The end-to-end journey

One homeowner, from the first contact to a system producing power. Each step below is either done by the system on its own, or done by a person with the system checking the work.

Sale

1 · Contact arrivesCreate Contact, a dealer lead, or a web form. Duplicates caught on every channel; owner set to the creator.
2 · AppointmentScheduled, rescheduled or no-show on Contact stages; every move logged.
3 · Quote & financeDeal with versioned proposals; financing approved.
4 · Contract signedSigning form, then system recorded, project created at Survey, contact held.

Delivery

5 · SurveySite survey; homeowner auto-invited; rating asked on completion.
6 · DesignPlans, shading report, engineering stamps.
7 · PermitsBuilding permit, ICA, HOA, NTP in parallel tracks.
8 · ProcurementMaterial ordered and delivered.
9 · InstallScheduled; 48 h and 24 h reminders; photos uploaded.
10 · Inspection & PTOInspection passed; utility PTO; "power on" push.
11 · CompleteTotal days, final Drive Updated, last rating.

At every step: the homeowner sees the current stage in plain language with its typical duration; the PM sees cards ageing against thresholds; the dealer sees the project move in their portal; each stage change is written to history and the activity log; and the dashboard and reports reflect it immediately.

Chapter 12

AI integration — current state

The product contains no calls to an AI model today. That was deliberate: phase one made the data right first, because AI working on wrong or scattered data is worse than no AI. The schema was designed from day one so that phase two needs no database redesign. Chapter 16 lists each AI capability to add; chapter 17 orders them into phases.

Why the platform is well placed for AI

Typed, structured stage data

Every stage field is a real column with a known type, so an AI's answer can be written into a column and validated, not pasted into a notes box.

Rules the AI cannot break

Stage validation, the hold, the won-deal lock and row-level security are all in the database. An AI agent acting through the same API gets exactly the same refusals as a person.

A place for doubt

The exceptions queue gives AI somewhere to put work it is not sure of, which is what lets it act alone on everything else.

A full audit trail

Every action is logged with actor and kind, so AI actions can be attributed, reviewed and reversed like anyone else's.
