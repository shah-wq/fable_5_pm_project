Chapter 9 · Module catalogue

CRM & sales modules

The half of the business that happens before anybody signs: every person you have ever spoken to, where each one stands, the moment they sign, and the sale followed through to a finished install.

M21CRM foundationLive

Migrations 003300, 003400 · Sales role · the People model

  • One person, one record: clients is the spine. A person moves from prospect to customer with no copying: their lifecycle (lead, prospect, customer, past customer) is derived from the projects that exist, never typed by hand.
  • Channels: every email and phone a person has, normalised and matched, with one primary per kind kept in sync with the person's main columns. The duplicate check searches all of them, so a second address is still the same person.
  • Addresses: mailing and property addresses kept separately, so a person with two houses has two deals rather than two records.
  • Sales role with deal visibility (own, team, all), a "manage all deals" capability, and the unassigned pool every rep can work.
  • Activity kinds on the audit log (call, email, SMS, meeting, note, form…) linked to deal and client, so a logged call "touches" the person.
  • Dealer submissions (the old leads table) became deals with their attribution and a stage, and a compatibility view keeps old readers working.

  • Signing is a step, not a drag. Nothing moves until the form is saved, and Cancel leaves the contact where they were. The form opens pre-filled from their open deal (a 5.4 kW quote arrives as 5.4) and from their dealer and mailing address.
  • The project is created automatically through the same conversion the deal board uses: system size, brands, battery, module count and contract value are copied; the deal's documents gain the project link; the deal becomes Won. A contact with no deal gets one, at Contract out. Any refusal leaves nothing behind.
  • The hold: a database trigger refuses every path out of Contract signed (board, Lead status, SQL) while the project exists. The board shows the card locked with its project link, and the record explains why.
  • Delete project (admin only, with the project code typed to confirm) unwinds the sale. The project's stages, tasks, messages and forms are removed. The deal is reopened at Contract out with its system, the deal's documents such as the signed agreement are kept, and the activity log is kept. The contact is then free to move.
  • Only the system, usage, money, dealer and address columns can be written by signing, whatever the request carries, and whole-number fields are rounded instead of failing.
M25DealsLive

Migration 003500, 004200 · /deals, /deals/new, /deals/[id] · lib/deals

  • Board: the project stages Survey → Complete with Hold and Cancelled. It is the Pipeline's own board over the projects deals became, so the two can never disagree. It updates live: a change in another tab arrives in under a second, and anyone else's within 10 seconds, with no reload. A note points to deals not yet signed.
  • Sales reps follow their sales here view-only (the project team moves projects), and their cards open the deal.
  • Table: every deal, signed or not, in the deal's own stages (New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Contract out → Won / Lost), with probability (computed or set by hand), next action and due date, value, weighted pipeline and missing items.
  • Deal record: qualification, logged activity (a voicemail is an attempt and does not move the deal; a real conversation with a next action does), versioned proposals (the newest sets the price), Solar details, documents.
  • Rules in the database: New cannot advance without two-way contact; a skip is allowed only when every skipped stage is satisfied; Won requires a signed contract and creates the project; a won deal cannot be un-won (except by an admin deleting its project); Lost needs a reason from the list and can be reopened.

M27E-signature & change orders (PandaDoc)Live

Migrations 004400 · 004500 · lib/esign · /api/integrations/pandadoc/webhook

  • Contracts: the signing form offers Send for e-signature, by email or signed on screen with the homeowner present. The form is checked the way signing checks it before PandaDoc is called; nothing moves until the homeowner signs. When they sign, the same signing function runs with what was sent, as the rep who sent it: project created at Survey, contact held, signed PDF filed on the deal and the project.
  • Change orders: raised on the project with a reason and an amount (numbered from the company's CO prefix and counter), sent through PandaDoc or approved by hand, voided while still a draft. Signed or approved, the amount is added to the contract value once, and the signed PDF is linked from the change order.
  • Safety: webhooks need PandaDoc's HMAC signature; completion is idempotent; an outcome that cannot apply keeps its reason and a Finish button; one document out per subject. Contacts with a contract out show Awaiting e-signature.

M29NotificationsLive

Migration 004700 · lib/notify · bell in the sidebar, /notifications, /portal/updates, Admin → Notifications · delivered by /api/push/reminders

  • One catalogue of 55 kinds across homeowner (29), project manager (16), admin (3), sales rep (3) and dealer (5): project started, stage advanced, survey scheduled, permit approved, equipment delivered, inspection passed, PTO granted, payment requested, project assigned, permit expiring, install tomorrow not ready, document signed or declined, lead assigned, contact or deal going quiet, your project moved, commission payable, and so on. Each has in app, email and push switches and an On switch; off means never raised.
  • Raised by the database: triggers on projects, the seven stage forms, customer requests, documents, chat, e-sign, change orders, contacts, deals, ratings, commissions and exceptions. Time-based kinds (ageing past threshold, permits expiring within the warning window, tomorrow's installs missing a permit / materials / a signed change order, quiet contacts and deals) are raised by the scheduled job. Deduped on kind, recipient and key, so nothing is said twice.
  • Delivered every ten minutes: the words are rendered once for feed, email and push; email unless the homeowner opted out; push to registered devices; homeowners' rows wait through quiet hours, staff's do not. A homeowner without a login is emailed.
  • Seen: a bell with the unread count on every staff and dealer screen and a feed page; an Updates card and page in the homeowner's portal and app. Every row records when it was emailed, pushed and read.
M30AI automation — document reader, reply drafts, briefings, auto-advanceLive · needs key

Migration 004800 · lib/ai · /exceptions, Admin → AI automation · runs from uploads, chat and /api/push/reminders

  • Document reader: every PDF or photo attached to a stage form is read by the model, which proposes values for that stage's fields with a confidence and the words that support each — permit number, approval and expiry dates, PO number and delivery date, final system size. Proposals wait as suggestions on the stage form and in the exceptions queue; Accept writes through the form's own allowlist, Reject writes nothing. With auto-apply on, values above the admin's threshold are written at once. A file that is not what it was filed as becomes a high-severity exception.
  • Exceptions queue: a screen over the exceptions table for everything the automation would not decide alone, with the evidence and Accept / Reject on each suggestion; acknowledge, dismiss, resolve. Resolving closes undecided suggestions unapplied; deciding the last suggestion resolves its exception. A new exception raises the PM's "AI needs a decision" notification.
  • Evidence-based auto-advance: no model. For each stage the admin ticks, a complete project moves on through the same move service as the green button — same gate, notification, chat line and audit entry — attributed to the automation's own service account; held while a suggestion is undecided.
  • Reply drafts: a homeowner's message gets a draft built from the project's facts, above the PM's composer to send, edit or dismiss. Money, cancellations, complaints and dates not yet set always need a person. With auto-send on, a confident draft goes out as the PM, signed as automatic.
  • Morning briefing: at the chosen hour, Ask SolarFlow writes each PM's day from the PM report under their own permissions, delivered as a notification and email.
  • Plumbing: a job queue in the database (claimed, retried with delay, failed after three attempts), run right after an upload or message and by the scheduled job; every switch defaults to the cautious side; every read, decision and automatic action is logged with the tokens used.
